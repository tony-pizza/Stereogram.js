Stereogram Text Crawl

Any amount of text, crawling up and away into the distance, hidden in a moving stereogram.

Paste as much text as you like. Blank lines separate paragraphs.

Paste this into any web page to show just the stereogram, with this text and these settings. It needs stereogram.min.js and depthmappers/TextCrawlDepthMapper.js from Stereogram.js, uploaded next to the page.

How do I see it?

Bring your face close to the screen and look "through" it, as if focusing on something far away, then slowly lean back while keeping your eyes relaxed. The dots will start to overlap and the text will appear floating above a tilted plane. If you're stuck, turn on Show depth map to see what you should be looking for.

While paused, scroll over the crawl with your mouse wheel or trackpad to move the text back and forth. Space bar pauses.
