How do I see it?

Bring your face close to the screen and look "through" it, as if focusing on something far away, then slowly lean back while keeping your eyes relaxed. Once the image locks into 3D, it stays locked while it moves. Turn on Show depth map to see what you should be looking for. Space bar pauses.

With Stable pattern on, the dots hold still and only the ones the changing depth needs are redrawn, so the shapes seem to move through a fixed texture. Switch it off and every frame becomes brand new random static: you can only see the movie in 3D, because no single frame shows anything.

What's a depth video?

A grayscale video where brightness means distance: white is near, black is far. Depth-estimation tools can make one from ordinary footage, and 3D software can render one directly. If your file shows the original footage and its depth side by side, use Use to pick the half with the depth map, and if near things are dark, tick Dark is near. You can also drop a video file onto the picture.
